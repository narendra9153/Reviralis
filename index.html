Ingredients Of Reviralis Complete

🍃 Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a water-soluble antioxidant that the body cannot make on its own, so it must come from food or supplements. It supports normal immune function, helps form collagen for skin and connective tissue, and improves the absorption of non-heme iron. Because it is not stored in large amounts, regular intake matters, which is why it is a staple in daily multi-nutrient formulas.

🍃 Vitamin D

Vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium and supports bone health, muscle function, and normal immune activity. Many adults get less than they need, especially people who spend most of the day indoors or live in areas with limited sunlight. Including it in a daily capsule is a simple way to help cover that gap alongside food and safe sun exposure.

🍃 Zinc

Zinc is a trace mineral involved in hundreds of enzyme reactions. It contributes to normal immune function, wound healing, protein synthesis, and the senses of taste and smell. The body does not store zinc well, so a steady daily supply is useful. Because very high doses can cause problems, it is important to stick to the recommended serving.

🍃 Selenium

Selenium is a trace mineral that supports antioxidant enzyme systems and normal thyroid function. It works alongside vitamin E in protecting cells from oxidative stress. Only small amounts are needed, and food sources such as nuts, fish, and eggs provide it, but supplements can help people whose diets are low in selenium-rich foods.

🍃 Green Tea Leaf Extract (EGCG)

Green tea extract is a concentrated source of catechins, especially EGCG, a polyphenol studied for its antioxidant properties. It has been researched in relation to metabolism, heart health, and cellular protection. Extracts used in supplements are usually standardized so that each capsule delivers a consistent amount of active compounds.

🍃 Grape Seed Extract

Grape seed extract contains oligomeric proanthocyanidins, plant compounds with strong antioxidant activity in laboratory studies. It has been investigated for circulation and blood vessel support, though more large human trials are needed. In a formula like Reviralis Complete, it adds to the overall antioxidant profile alongside vitamins C and E.

🍃 Olive Leaf Powder (Oleuropein)

Olive leaf has a long history in traditional Mediterranean wellness practices. Its main compound, oleuropein, is a polyphenol with antioxidant activity. Research is still developing, and results from studies vary, so it is best viewed as a supportive botanical that complements, rather than replaces, healthy habits and medical care.

🍃 Magnesium

Magnesium takes part in more than 300 reactions in the body, including muscle and nerve function, energy production, and normal heart rhythm. Many people do not reach recommended intakes through diet alone. It is commonly included in wellness formulas to support relaxation, steady energy, and a normal response to physical and mental stress.

🍃 Bacopa Monnieri

Bacopa is an herb used for centuries in Ayurvedic tradition and is now widely studied for memory and cognitive function. Some trials suggest that regular use over eight to twelve weeks may support attention and recall, though findings are not uniform. Bacopa can cause mild stomach upset in some people, so taking it with food is wise.

🍃 B Vitamins (B1, B2, B3, B5, B6)

The B-complex vitamins work as a team to help convert food into energy and keep the nervous system functioning normally. Vitamin B6 also supports normal mood-related neurotransmitter production, and B5 contributes to stress-related hormone balance. Since they are water-soluble, the body needs a regular supply, which makes them well suited to a daily supplement.

🍃 Cinnamon Bark Extract

Cinnamon bark extract contains polyphenols that have been studied for their role in healthy blood sugar metabolism. Results from trials are mixed, but it remains a popular ingredient in metabolic support blends. People who take medication for blood sugar should check with a healthcare provider before using any supplement containing it.
